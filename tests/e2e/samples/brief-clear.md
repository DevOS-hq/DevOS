# Invoice export: brief

## The problem

- [Finance staff at our customers copy invoices out of the billing page by hand at month end](spec.md#summary). One accountant spends two days on it every month, and copy mistakes reach her ledger.

## The goal

- [An admin can download a whole month of invoices as one spreadsheet file](spec.md#goals).
- [Half as many support tickets about invoices at month end](spec.md#goals).

## The plan

- [First, a download button on the billing page for one month's invoices, due 2026-12-04](spec.md#milestone-1-csv-download).
- [Then an email that sends the same file to an admin on the first of each month](spec.md#milestone-2-scheduled-export).

## Not doing

- [No direct link to accounting software such as QuickBooks or Xero](spec.md#non-goals).

## Still open

- [Should refunds appear as their own rows in the file?](spec.md#open-questions)
