# Checkout Revamp

- **Priority:** P1
- **Stakeholders:** Payments team, Support team
- **Target quarter:** 2026 Q4

## Requirements

### Guest checkout

- **Type:** need
- **Stakeholders:**
  - Payments team
  - Growth team
- **Description:** Shoppers can pay without creating an account.
  The order confirmation page offers to create an account afterwards.

### Saved cards

- **Type:** want
- **Stakeholders:** none
- **Description:** Returning shoppers can pay with a card saved on an earlier order.

## Notes

The old checkout stays live behind a flag until the new one serves all traffic.
